Services

What I bring
to your organisation

CB Consulting & Management is an operational and strategic advisory firm — not a law firm. All engagements are management consulting mandates: I work alongside your leadership team and, where needed, coordinate with your legal counsel to ensure execution is both commercially sound and compliant.

01

Tax & Regulatory Advisory

Operational strategy on pan-European VAT, excise duties, and customs. Translating regulatory change into concrete business decisions before it hits the bottom line. Multi-jurisdiction audit supervision and compliance transformation — handled as a business challenge, not a legal matter.

02

Interim Management

Stepping in as an operational Tax Director, General Counsel, or Compliance lead during transitions, restructurings, or critical growth phases. I bring the depth of a seasoned in-house executive with the flexibility of an independent adviser — deployed quickly, without overhead.

03

Board & C-Suite Advisory

Strategic counsel to Boards and Executive Committees on regulatory risk, fiscal governance, and technology-driven compliance transformation. I convert complex risk landscapes into actionable decisions — and help leadership move with confidence when speed matters.

04

E-Commerce & Digital Transformation

End-to-end operational structuring for domestic and international e-commerce and distance selling businesses. E-invoicing mandate implementation, cross-border compliance frameworks, and AI-driven tax technology integration — always from a business-first perspective.

My distinctive strength lies in simplifying complexity at scale — turning highly complex tax, regulatory, and cross-border environments into clear, actionable decisions that create real operational impact. I transform regulation into a growth enabler, not a constraint.
